Source documents

The original files behind the electoral bonds, mining and party donation releases

These are the original files behind the electoral bonds, mining and party donation releases, exactly as they were downloaded from the Election Commission of India, MSTC, the Ministry of Coal and the Odisha Directorate of Mines.

Government websites change. Files get moved, renamed or taken down, and a dataset is only as good as your ability to go back to where it came from. So every source file is kept here unchanged, with a SHA-256 checksum, and every row in the other releases names the file (and for the donations, the page) it was read from.

Where they came from

  • Election Commission of India: Disclosure of Electoral Bonds, and the contribution, electoral trust and expenditure reports on the Commission's website.
  • MSTC: mineral auction results.
  • Ministry of Coal: tranche-wise allocation of coal mines.
  • Directorate of Mines, Odisha: lessee register, one page per district.

Everything was fetched from public pages. Nothing needed a login.

A note on personal data

Some of the Election Commission's Form 24A and electoral trust reports print the donors' PAN numbers and addresses. They are kept here unchanged because they are the Commission's own public documents and the evidence for everything else. The donation table built from them leaves both out.
